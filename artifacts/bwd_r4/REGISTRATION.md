## Known capability constraints at registration

The real BP1 graph census is 317 names, four tail-only and 313 on path. Twelve of the 313 are InstanceNorm scale/bias initializers reused through ONNX Identity aliases. They are identity values and BP1 differentiates them. R3’s frozen tc_signal requires them to be frozen when recording. The harness will attempt the ordered all-weight path and explicitly gate this refusal on a real initializer and Voice’s own AdaIN call. Replacing the frozen package or freezing any of these parameters would require changing the acceptance configuration. The stock weight and graph choices, constant replay, G0 cross-check rule, and memory admission estimate are recorded here before comparison.
