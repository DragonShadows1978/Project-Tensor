Tolerances (absolute + relative, chosen before GPU): float32 forward (3e-5,3e-5), backward (3e-4,3e-4); float16 forward (0.02,0.02), backward (0.06,0.06); bfloat16 forward (0.12,0.12), backward (0.35,0.35). These compare to float64 reference evaluated on representable input/parameter/projection values. Maximum group n=72; CUDA sums accumulate float32, so gamma_71 <=4.24e-6. Reductions store in operand dtype; unit roundoffs u=2**-24,2**-11,2**-8. For non-singleton random groups require variance >=1/64, |input|<=2.25, affine <=1.5; cancellations in centering and gradient motivate absolute floors. Forward budgets cover roughly 40u (half), 30u (bf16), plus float32 reduction and centering conditioning; backward covers roughly 120u (half), 90u (bf16), graph accumulation and projection cancellation. Float32 budgets allow ~7 times gamma_71 forward and ~70 times backward, plus near-eps inverse std <=46 and bounded projection. These are finite test-domain engineering error budgets, not universal error theorems. Singleton variance/center are exact zero. No tolerance adjustment based on GPU permitted.

CPU hand reference atol=rtol=0 (binary exact normalization denominator constructed as 1 or 2). Central finite differences h=2**-18, atol=rtol=2e-7: O(h**2) truncation with eps>=0.125, O(eps64/h) cancellation on small bounded examples leaves >100x margin.
